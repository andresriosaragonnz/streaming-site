    Privacy and tracking

    The modern web is built on the idea of CONSENT. That the user should agree to the terms in which a site will use their data.

    We want to go beyond this and apply the concept of 'JUSTIFICATION'. A site should have a reason to ask users for their data. And since a user will NOT get a better viewing experience by giving up their personal information, we just wont ask for it.

    As a user or this site you will get the full featured experience without needing an account. And without giving up any of your personal data.

    In the same way that a vinil or cassete does not need a user account to be listened to. Streaming music listening should remain private and untracked.

    So we wont have any tracking or analytics taken from visitors. And you can see that by checking the network tab in your browser and seeing that no tracking scripts are added to the site.

    Our guarantee

    In the brief history of the internet we have seen platforms start with beautiful intentions and then be corrupted and bought by big coorporations.

    This leads to bad experience for the end users who end up having to suddenly be squeezed for money or having their privacy violated and their data mined.

    We are protected by a very simple but effective strategy: Every time the artist downloads their videos from the private pages. We attach the whole source code for the site and a json file that maps all the data in the platform.

    So if our team gets bought out or corrupted in any way. The community has the power to rebuild the whole platform and recover all the data with just a few clicks

    Your responsibility

    As an artist you are part of our music history. So when you donwload your videos from this site make sure to keep them.

    That way if the community ever need to rebuild the site they will reach out to you to recover the videos and reauthorize them for the public.
